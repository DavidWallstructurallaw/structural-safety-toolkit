# Theory source index

The project draws on the following works by **Xiangyu Guo**. This index describes how the supplied papers informed engineering choices. It does not certify their broader empirical or mathematical claims. Page numbers refer to physical PDF pages in the supplied copies.

The source PDFs are not included in this repository. A software license does not grant rights to redistribute those papers. No DOI, publication date, or version is supplied where the project has not established it.

| Source | Supplied version / extent | Engineering use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| *The Structural Safety Problem of AI* | Version 1.0, 2026-09-12; 14 pages. | Execution topology, task authorization, boundary obligations, independent constraints, and distinctions among declarations, modeled paths, and observed effects. Fields, algorithms, and SS rule identifiers are project definitions. |
| *Responsibility Before Permission* | 12 pages; emphasis pp. 1–7 and 9–11. | Responsibility tied to operational control and correction. Its contextual examples do not become universal time limits or regulatory requirements. |
| *The Feedback-and-Responsibility Standard for AI-Assisted Work* | Supplied as *The Governance of Human Capacity in the AI Age*, Vol. 4; 20 pages. | Checkable basis, validation reliability, reviewer competence, consequence windows, and effective stopping authority. The toolkit does not certify expert qualifications. |
| *No AI Is Responsibility-Free: The Responsibility Topology of Consumer Interaction, Cognitive Integrity, and High-Consequence Systems* | 33 pages; emphasis pp. 5–10, 18–24, and 25–29. | Use-specific responsibility and distinctions among capability, output authority, and action authorization. Execution checks do not evaluate all cognitive or training-source duties. |
| *The Boundary Vacuum Law: Gradient, Boundary Failure, Topological Flow, and Pressure Capture in Social Systems* | Filename identifies v2; 29 pages. | Bypass routes, boundary failures, transferred burdens, and the difference between reachability and sustainable capacity. Social equations are not applied numerically to the execution graph. |
| *The Burnout of Possibility: Capability-Bandwidth Mismatch in the Early AI Era* | Supplied as *The Governance of Human Capacity in the AI Age*, Vol. 1; 16 pages. | Governance load and capacity limits. Conceptual ratios do not become calibrated individual bandwidth scores. |
| *Evaluation Closure: Benchmark Inbreeding and the Design of Open AI Evaluation* | 2026; 16 pages; no version stated in the supplied copy. | Validation appropriate to the claimed ability, mechanism differences, and limits of closed synthetic evaluation. Repeated variants do not establish new mechanism coverage or universal safety probabilities. |

Seven papers were reviewed during Phase 0, totaling 140 pages. Source review and internal design review are distinct from external validation. The current software implements P1-1 input validation only; planned analysis and experiments are described in [semantics](semantics.md) and [experiments](experiments.md).
